About

Computer Engineering graduate from Mae Fah Luang University. Most of my time goes into MFU SOS, the university's emergency incident platform.

Someone in trouble opens it; someone on duty has to act on what they see. That constraint decides every technical call in the system.

My teammate was pulled onto other work early on, so I built most of it myself. He later restructured the codebase, and rebuilding my features onto his architecture taught me more about system design than the first version did.
